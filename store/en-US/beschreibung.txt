The YSKAR Wallet is the wallet for YSR, the currency of the YSKAR blockchain. Send, receive, contacts and history – clearly laid out, no detours.

YOUR KEY STAYS WITH YOU
• Your wallet is created on this device. Nobody at YSKAR has access to your key.
• 12 recovery words – use them to restore your wallet on any device.
• The words are stored encrypted in Android's protected storage and never go into cloud backups.

SEND AND RECEIVE
• Enter an address, pick a contact or scan a QR code.
• Before sending you see recipient, amount and network fee once more – then slide and confirm.
• Receive with a QR code or your address, optionally requesting a specific amount.
• Amounts exact to the 8th decimal place.

CONTACTS AND HISTORY
• Contacts with names – all payments with one person in a single, clear history.
• Pending payments and confirmations at a glance.
• Optional notification for every incoming payment.
• Contacts stay on your device.

SECURITY
• PIN and fingerprint.
• Auto-lock after a time you choose.
• No screenshots while the 12 words are on screen.

SIGN AND SIGN IN
• Prove with a signed message that an address is yours, and verify other people's signatures.
• Sign in to YSKAR sites with a QR code. A signature cannot move YSR.

EASY TO LEARN
• A short 8-step introduction explains every feature.
• English and German.

No ads, no tracking, no account with us.
